STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-ROT-118
Rotating Equipment: Vibration Monitoring & Bearing Health
Revision: 2  |  Effective Date: 2026-02-01  |  Owner: Reliability Dept.

1. SCOPE
   Covers pumps, fans, and motors on rigid foundations (ISO 10816-3 Group 2,
   medium machines 15 kW to 300 kW). Pump P-201 (crude charge pump) falls in
   this class.

2. VIBRATION MEASUREMENT
   - Measure overall velocity in mm/s RMS at the bearing housings, in three
     axes (horizontal, vertical, axial), at normal operating speed.
   - Report the highest of the three axes as the machine's vibration level.

3. VIBRATION LIMITS (velocity, mm/s RMS)
   - Zone A/B (good):        up to 4.5 mm/s  — normal operation, no action.
   - Zone C (unsatisfactory): 4.5 - 7.1 mm/s — acceptable short-term only;
     increase monitoring to weekly; plan corrective maintenance.
   - Zone D (unacceptable):  above 7.1 mm/s — SAFETY-CRITICAL. Vibration at or
     above 7.1 mm/s RMS exceeds the allowable limit; schedule inspection and
     prepare for shutdown. Do not continue running unattended.
   The 7.1 mm/s RMS boundary is the maximum allowable limit for this machine
   class per ISO 10816-3.

4. BEARING TEMPERATURE
   - Bearing housing temperature normal range: up to 80 degC.
   - Alarm at 90 degC; trip at 100 degC.

5. PIPE / SHELL WALL THICKNESS (associated static equipment)
   - Minimum allowable wall thickness for in-service vessels and lines in this
     unit: 6.35 mm (0.25 in), per the vessel datasheet and SOP-MNT-402.
   - Any measured thickness below 6.35 mm is BELOW MINIMUM: remove from service
     pending engineering (fitness-for-service) review.
   - Margin under 10% above minimum: shorten the next inspection interval.

6. ESCALATION
   On any Zone D vibration (>= 7.1 mm/s) or bearing trip (>= 100 degC):
   a. Notify the control room and Reliability Engineer immediately.
   b. Prepare an orderly shutdown of the affected machine per unit procedure.
   c. Log the event with tag number, axis, reading, and timestamp.
