STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Feed Preheat Train — Main Feed Control Valve (FCV-105) Operation & Safety Limits
Document No.: SOP-JAM-CDU2-104-A | Revision: 3 | Effective Date: 01-Feb-2026 | Site: Jamnagar Refinery Complex, Gujarat
1. Purpose
This procedure defines the safe operating limits, monitoring requirements, and emergency response actions for the Main Feed Control Valve (FCV-105) and associated equipment in the Crude Unit-2 Feed Preheat Train (Unit 100) at Jamnagar Refinery Complex, as shown on Drawing RIL-JAM-CDU2-104 Rev. C.
2. Scope
This SOP applies to all board operators, field operators, and maintenance personnel working on or near P-101, E-102, FCV-105, PSV-102, and H-101 within Unit 100, Jamnagar site.

4. Normal Operating Procedure
4.1 Startup
a) Confirm P-101 suction and discharge valves are open and casing is vented of vapor.
b) Start P-101 and confirm flow indication FI-101 is above minimum flow (450 gpm) within 60 seconds to protect against pump surge.
c) Slowly open FCV-105 in manual mode, ramping no faster than 5% valve position per minute, while monitoring PI-105.
d) Once PI-105 stabilizes below 285 psig and flow is steady, transfer FCV-105 to automatic control.
e) Bring H-101 online per SOP-JAM-CDU2-104-C (Fired Heater Startup) once feed flow through the preheat train is confirmed stable.
4.2 Normal Monitoring
Board operators shall log PI-105, FI-101, and H-101 outlet temperature every 2 hours during normal operation. Any reading within 5% of a safe operating limit shall be reported to the Shift Supervisor immediately.
5. Emergency Response Actions
Condition
Trigger
Required Action
High Pressure
PI-105 > 285 psig
Close FCV-105 from the board; notify Shift Supervisor; do not reset until root cause identified.
Relief Lift
PSV-102 discharge alarm
Initiate emergency shutdown of P-101 per SOP-JAM-CDU2-104-D; isolate E-102.
High Temperature
H-101 outlet > 720 °F
Reduce firing rate immediately; if temperature continues to rise, trip H-101 fuel gas per Fired Heater ESD procedure.
Low Flow
FI-101 < 450 gpm for > 60 sec
Verify P-101 minimum flow bypass is open; if flow does not recover, stop P-101 to prevent pump damage.
6. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Fire-resistant coveralls, hard hat, safety glasses, and steel-toe boots are required in Unit 100 at all times. H2S monitors are mandatory when working within 10 meters of the crude feed lines. Hearing protection is required within 3 meters of P-101 while running.
